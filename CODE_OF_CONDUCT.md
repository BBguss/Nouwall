# Code of Conduct

nouwall follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussions
about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not
accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer
privately at **mbbbdg@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules.

The enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
